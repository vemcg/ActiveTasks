ActiveTasks

A to-do list, prioritized the Eisenhower way.
ActiveTasks is a companion to MicroTasking: a traditional to-do list that reads the same Google Sheet as MicroTasking. Items don't just appear here from every checked row - you refer a task to ActiveTasks from MicroTasking's own task queue, dragging a finger across an importance/urgency matrix to set its priority as you send it over.
1
Install & Launch the App Android Only
  1. Scan or Download: Point your phone's camera at the QR code below, or tap its download link if you are viewing this page on your phone:

    Download ActiveTasks APK (v0.1.0-11)
    Open the download again
  2. Allow Sideloading (First Time Only):
    • After tapping Download anyway, open your browser's Downloads list or the Android Files app and tap the downloaded .apk file. The browser may not open the installer automatically.
    • If Android displays "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source", tap Settings, toggle Allow from this source to ON, then go back and tap Install.
    • If Google Play Protect displays a warning, tap More details → Install anyway.
2
Point It at Your MicroTasking Google Sheet

ActiveTasks doesn't need a spreadsheet of its own — it reads the same one MicroTasking already uses. If you already have MicroTasking set up:

  1. Get your two setup QR codes (Sheet URL and Apps Script Web App URL) from Step 3 of the MicroTasking onboarding page. That section also explains how to deploy the Web App and find its URL — it's the same two codes for both apps, so if you already made them for MicroTasking, reuse them.
  2. Open ActiveTasks on your Android phone, open Settings and tap Scan Sheet QR Code and Scan Web App QR Code to scan the two codes (each is filed by what it contains, so a mix-up can't overwrite the other box). Or paste the two URLs into the boxes by hand.
  3. Tap Sync Lists.
3
Refer a Task from MicroTasking

ActiveTasks starts empty on purpose — a task only shows up here once you send it over from MicroTasking:

  1. In MicroTasking, open the task queue.
  2. Tap Refer to ActiveTasks on any task, any time — it doesn't matter whether you've started it yet.
  3. Drag/tap the importance × urgency matrix that pops up to set its priority. That task leaves MicroTasking's queue and lands in ActiveTasks.
  4. Back in ActiveTasks, swipe between your lists (one per Sheet tab) to find it, set a progress %, and mark it Complete (for now) to hand it back to MicroTasking, or Fully complete to remove it for good.
